Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

EEB Consultancy LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates AI Voice Changer: Sound FX (the "App"). Core voice, audio, text-to-speech, and video processing is performed locally on your device. The App does not require a user account, and we do not operate a backend server that receives your recordings, videos, or text.

The free version of the App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising services may process certain device, technical, and usage information as described below. Premium subscribers do not receive advertisements within the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, contacts, or profile information to use the App.

We do not collect or upload your voice recordings, processed audio, videos, or text entered into the App to our servers.

However, third-party advertising services integrated into the App, including Google AdMob, may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from network information
  • Device model, operating system, and application information
  • Advertising interactions, impressions, and performance information
  • Diagnostic and technical information associated with advertising services
  • Consent preferences and privacy-related signals

The information processed depends on the SDK configuration, applicable law, device settings, and consent choices. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Voice Recordings and Audio Processing

When you choose to record your voice, the App requests access to your device's microphone.

Voice recordings and voice effects are processed locally on your device. Your recordings are not uploaded to EEB Consultancy LLC or to a third-party voice-processing server.

Temporary audio files may be created on your device while recording or processing audio. If you choose to save a recording, it may remain stored locally within the App until you delete it or uninstall the App.

3. Voice Effects and Voice Transformation

Voice transformation features are performed locally using digital audio processing technologies.

The App does not create biometric voice profiles, identify users by their voice, or create personalized voice-cloning models from your recordings. Your recordings are not used by EEB Consultancy LLC to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Text to Speech

The Text to Speech feature uses Apple's system speech technologies. Text entered into this feature is not sent to a server operated by EEB Consultancy LLC.

If you choose to save generated speech within the App, limited text associated with that recording, such as part of the entered text used as a title, may be stored locally on your device.

5. Video Voice Effects

You may select a video using Apple's system media picker. Selected media is processed locally on your device.

The App may extract the video's audio, apply a selected voice effect, and combine the processed audio with the video. Your videos and their audio are not uploaded to our servers.

Processed videos may be stored locally within the App when you choose to save them.

6. Reverse Voice

Reverse Voice processing occurs locally on your device. Audio used with this feature is not transmitted to EEB Consultancy LLC or to a remote processing service.

7. Prank Soundboard

Prank Soundboard sounds are included with the App and are played locally on your device. Using the soundboard does not transmit your recordings to us.

Advertising services may process technical or advertising-related information when advertisements are requested or displayed, independently of the soundboard's local audio playback.

8. Local Storage and Deletion

Content that you choose to save, including processed audio and videos, may be stored locally within the App's storage on your device.

Saved items can be deleted through the App's History functionality. Temporary files may also be created while content is being processed, and iOS may manage temporary storage according to its own policies.

Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored application data from your device.

Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, locally stored App data may be included in backups managed by Apple, including iCloud device backups. EEB Consultancy LLC does not operate or control Apple's backup services.

Advertising-related data processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. Deleting locally saved App content does not necessarily delete data held by third parties.

9. Sharing Content

The App allows you to share certain audio and video content using Apple's system sharing interface. Sharing occurs only when you initiate it.

If you choose to share content with another application or service, that third party's privacy practices apply to the content you choose to share. We do not track which third-party services you use for sharing.

10. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Purchases, billing, subscription management, and payment processing are handled by Apple through StoreKit.

EEB Consultancy LLC does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Premium subscribers receive an advertisement-free experience within the App. The App does not request or display AdMob advertisements for users whose Premium entitlement has been verified as active.

Apple may independently process purchase and subscription information according to its own privacy practices.

Google AdMob and its associated advertising technologies may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location information, advertising events, technical information, and other data necessary for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related services.

UMP consent and ATT authorization are separate mechanisms. The App respects applicable privacy choices and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking without the required authorization.

You can manage Apple's tracking permission through your device's Settings. Where required and supported, the App provides access to advertising privacy options for reviewing or changing applicable consent choices.

We do not operate a separate third-party behavioral analytics platform for the App. Google AdMob may nevertheless provide advertising measurement and diagnostic information.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

Information about Google's use of data from partner applications is available at: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

12. Internet and External Services

The App's core voice, audio, reverse-audio, text-to-speech, and video-processing features are designed to operate without sending your content to a server operated by EEB Consultancy LLC.

Internet access may be required to request and display advertisements, obtain advertising consent information, access Apple services such as App Store purchases and subscription restoration, or open external pages such as this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, or the App Store.

13. Third-Party and Open-Source Technologies

The App uses Apple system frameworks and open-source audio-processing technologies to provide functionality such as audio effects, pitch and formant transformation, time stretching, and sound processing.

These locally integrated audio-processing components do not independently send your recordings to a backend operated by EEB Consultancy LLC.

The App also integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform SDK for advertising and consent management. These services may communicate with Google's systems and process information as described in this Policy.

The App is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the App.
